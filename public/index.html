What is FantasyLoL?

FantasyLoL brings the thrill of fantasy sports to competitive League of Legends. Instead of real-world athletes, you draft professional LoL players from the pro circuit and assemble your own fantasy roster.

As the pros compete in live tournaments, their in-game performance — kills, deaths, assists, creep score, wins, and more — is converted into fantasy points for the players on your team. The better your drafted pros play, the more points you earn.

Create or join leagues with friends, compete week to week, and climb the leaderboard to prove you know the pro scene better than anyone else. Whether you follow the LCK, LEC, LCS, or any other region, FantasyLoL turns every match into a chance to win.
